I am **Junteng Liu**, a first-year Ph.D. candidate in the HKUST NLP Group at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, supervised by Professor Junxian He. Before joining HKUST, I completed my B.Eng. at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, where Professor Junxian He also advised me during my undergraduate studies. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.
